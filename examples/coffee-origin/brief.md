# The Coffee Highlands

Tell the Ethiopian highland legend of a young goat herder who sees her goats
dance after eating red berries and shares the discovery that leads to the first
cup of coffee. Present it as folklore. Begin at dawn in the highlands and end
with a steaming cup shared in the village. Keep the tone warm and curious.

Use ten short, easily translated narration lines of roughly ten words each.
Give each line a distinct visual moment that live-action footage can show simply.
